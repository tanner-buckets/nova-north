NoVa North League

A trading card game league for players of every age and every skill level, meeting every Sunday at Continental Cards in Ashburn.

When and where we meet

Sundays at 2:00 PM

Sign-in closes at 2:30 PM. Arrive before then to play.

Continental Cards Tournament Location
21140 Ashburn Crossing Dr #110
Ashburn, VA 20147

A professor at the desk in the back will sign you in. If it is your first time, come a few minutes early so we can get you set up correctly.

What is coming

This page is the first piece of a larger site. The schedule, the prize wall, and the loyalty program are being built next.

Ask a professor at league in the meantime.

NoVa North League meets at Continental Cards and is run by volunteer professors.

This site is made and maintained by those volunteers. It is not an official publisher website.
