LoCo League

A Play! Pokémon League for players of every age and every skill level. Come to play, come to learn, come to trade.

Most weeks that means the Pokémon Trading Card Game. We also host other Play! Pokémon competitive events, including Pokémon Champions VGC and Pokémon GO.

When and where we meet

Sundays at 2:00 PM

Sign-in closes at 2:30 PM. Arrive before then to play.

Connect with us on Discord

The store runs a Discord for everyone who plays there. It is where pairings, last-minute changes and the week’s plans get posted, and where you can ask a question between Sundays.

Parents are welcome to join and keep an eye on it. It is the store’s server rather than ours, so it covers everything Continental Cards runs, not only league.

Your first visit

  1. Just turn up

    You do not need a Player ID to come to league. Bring yourself, and a professor at the desk in the back will get you signed in.

  2. You do not need to know how to play

    We will teach you. Plenty of people learn here, and you can borrow one of our rental decks rather than bringing your own. There is no charge to try one.

  3. Get a Player ID when you are ready

    A Player ID is free, takes about five minutes, and is what lets you register for tournaments and collect prize points. You can play without one, so there is no rush.

    How to find or create a Player ID, including for a child.

  4. Play, and have fun

    That is the whole point. Ask a professor about our programs while you are here.
