Printable lists

Loyalty tiers to hand to the store, and pre-registration to work from at the desk. Build one, then print the page.

Both sheets carry full names and Player IDs. That is a disclosure to the venue and to whoever runs the desk, not publication — nothing here is ever shown on a public page. Most of these players are children, so hand the sheet to a person rather than leaving it on a table.

Building a sheet opens the print dialog straight away. If you close it, the sheet stays below and your browser’s own print command works on it.

LoCo League meets at Continental Cards and is run by volunteer professors.
