Prize wall and earning

The two sides of the prize point ledger: what points buy, and what earns them. Both lists feed the points screen, and the prize wall is what players see on the prize items page.

Nothing here is ever deleted, only retired. Every point already awarded or spent still points at one of these rows, and anything retired can be brought back.
