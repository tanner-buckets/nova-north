Resources

The links we reach for most, in one place. Every one of them is an outside site and opens in a new tab.

Rules

  • Play! Pokémon rules and resources Every handbook and policy in one place: the Tournament Rules Handbook, the penalty guidelines, deck list forms, banned lists and the format documents for each game. Where to start when a question turns out to be a rules question. pokemon.com
  • Clipboard penalty sheet The penalties on one page, made to sit on a clipboard. Print it before league rather than looking it up in the middle of a round. pokemon.com · PDF

Running a league day

  • Play! Tools Where an event is created, and where it is reported once the day is over. play-tools.pokemon.com
  • vclock A full-screen countdown for round time, big enough to read from the far side of the room. vclock.com
  • Wheel of Names A spinning wheel for drawing a name at random. For a raffle or a door prize, where being seen to be fair matters as much as being fair. wheelofnames.com

Professors, and keeping up

  • Pokémon Professor Program The programme itself: joining it, professor ranks, and the account behind both. professorprogram.pokemon.com
  • Professor University The training and the exams for Play! Pokémon professors. professoruniversity.pokemon.com
  • PokéGym A long-running community forum: set news, card scans and deck talk. Run by players rather than by Pokémon. pokegym.net

The pokemon.com links are official. vclock, Wheel of Names and PokéGym are not — they are a timer, a raffle wheel and a forum that happen to be useful. Official documents get moved and renamed from time to time, so if one of these stops working, the rules and resources page above is where to find out where it went.

LoCo League meets at Continental Cards and is run by volunteer professors.
