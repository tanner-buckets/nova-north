Trainer Card Creator

Build a list of players, check their ranks, then print. Eight cards to a page, cut along the edges.

A card carries a full name and a birth year. That is the player’s own card, handed to the player — it is never shown on a public page, and this screen is the only place those two appear together. Most of these players are children, so hand a card to a person rather than leaving a stack on a table.

LoCo League meets at Continental Cards and is run by volunteer professors.
