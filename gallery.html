Send us a photo

Got a good one from a Sunday? Send it over and a professor will take a look. Nothing appears on this page until a professor has approved it, so it will not be here the moment you send it.

Please only send photos you took yourself, and ask first before sending one where somebody else’s child is recognisable. If a photo of you or your child is already up and you would like it taken down, tell a professor at league and it will come down the same day.

LoCo League meets at Continental Cards and is run by volunteer professors.

This site is made and maintained by those volunteers. It is not an official publisher website.
